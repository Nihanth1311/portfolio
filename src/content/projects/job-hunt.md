  - Tracks applications on a drag-and-drop board with notes, a timeline and automatic follow-up dates.
  - Sends a morning Telegram digest and instant alerts for strong matches.

Job hunting is mostly reading listings that were never a fit. Job Hunt does that reading for you. It gathers roles straight from company careers pages, keeps the ones that match your background, and prepares a résumé for each, so the time goes into applying.
